
The bone marrow niche develops late in embryonic development. 

red and white blood cells can be generated before the bone marrow niche formed. 

The site of blood cell generation shifts several times before moving to bone marrow niche. 

## In embroy development, when does the hematopoiesis happen in bone marrow niche


| stage                     | Timeframe            | Location               | Key Features                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Yolk Sac Phase**        | Begins around Day 7  | Yolk Sac               | -Primitive hematopoiesisProduces primitive erythroid cells, megakaryocytes, and macrophages                  |
| **Intra-embryonic Phase** | Around Day 27        | Aorta/Vitelline Artery | -Emergence of hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs)<br>- Transition to definitive hematopoiesis                    |
| **Fetal Liver Phase**     | Approximately Week 7 | Fetal Liver            | - Primary site for hematopoiesis<br>- Expansion and differentiation of HSCs into various blood cell lineages |
| **Bone Marrow Niche**     | Around Week 20       | Bone Marrow            | - Colonization by HSCs<br>- Becomes the main site for hematopoiesis at birth                                 |
