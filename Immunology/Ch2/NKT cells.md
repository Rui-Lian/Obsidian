|**spect**|**Innate Lymphoid Cells (ILCs)**|**T Cells**|
|---|---|---|
|**Origin**|Derived from common lymphoid progenitors|Derived from common lymphoid progenitors; mature in the thymus|
|**Activation**|Activated by cytokines and innate signals|Activated by antigen presentation via MHC and TCR engagement|
|**Speed of Response**|Rapid response (minutes to hours)|Slower response (days) due to clonal expansion and differentiation|
|**Subtypes**|ILC1, ILC2, ILC3|CD4+ (helper), CD8+ (cytotoxic), regulatory T cells, etc.|
|**Function**|Immediate defense against pathogens, tissue repair|Specific adaptive immune responses, memory formation|
