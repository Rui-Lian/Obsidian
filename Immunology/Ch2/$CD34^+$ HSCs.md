CD34 serves as a **unique marker** for identifying **undifferentiated hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs)** and **early progenitor cells** that can give rise to all blood cell lineages.

CD34 expression tends to **disappear** as hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) undergo further differentiation.

Role of CD34: 
- Cell adhesion and migration
- stem cell maintenance
