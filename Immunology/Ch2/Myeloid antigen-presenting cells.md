monocytes

macrophages: 
- Macro, big, 5-10 fold larger  
- two major origins: 
	- 1. circulating, derive from monocytes
	- 2. tissue resident, from embryonic cells
		- tissue specific functions
- Example of tissues with specific macrophages: brain, lung, liver, kidney, skin (langerhans cells), spleen, peritoneal, intestine, bone marrow, lymph node, heart. 

Dendritic: 
- potent antigen presenting to naive T cells.
