Humoral immunity is mediated by antibodies, which is produced by B cells and can be found in body fluid. 
- Antibody mediated
- B cell
- Body fluid, humoral

Cell-mediated immunity is mediated by *pathogen-specific* T lymphocytes. Pathogen can be eradicated directly bo cell immunity. Also, cellular immunity is aiding other cells in their work. 
- pathogen specific T cell
- Effector
- Helper
