
Four categories of Pathogens: 
- Viruses
- Bacteria
- Fungi
- Parasites

Pathogens must breach the natural barriers, e.g, acidity of stomach contents, vagina, perspiration, mucosa etc. 

Immune response is determined by types of pathogens and the environment that the pathogen is encountered. For examples, many bacterials are immune tolerated in GI but can cause severe infection if entered the blood.
