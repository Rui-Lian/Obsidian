#Innate #adaptive

- interconnected arms of immunity

Comparison: 

|                                | Innate                                              | Adaptive                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Response time                  | minutes to hours                                    | days                                                                   |
| Specificity                    | Limited: Fixed:                                     | Highly diverse, adapts to improve during the course of immune response |
| Response to repeated infection | same each time                                      | more rapid and effecitve with each subsequent exposure                 |
| Component                      | Barriers, phagocytes, pattern recognition molecules | T and B lymphocytes, antigen-specific receptors, antibodies.           |
